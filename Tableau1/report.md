## Visualization 1: Phosphorus measurements at four sites

![Phosphorus measurements at four sites](Visualizations/Viz1.png)

Rationale: The scatter plot helps me to compare individual phosphorus measurements across locations and time and display the variability present in the data. Therefore, the sampling gaps or high observations are visually easy to identify. It also prevents the average from concealing the important peaks. The horizontal X- axis displays the Sampling Date and the vertical Y-axis displays the phosphorus concentration and the 4 colors differentiate among the four sites. The unconnected points indicate that these samples were collected at different times. The 0.05 mg/L as a reference on the graph indicates a clear benchmark for finding measurements that were higher than the supplied upper guideline. Method: I utilised phosphorus measurements from the 4 monitoring sites between 2013-2016. Before plotting, I standardized the site names, excluded the duplicate records identified and also excluded the nine pH records. Plotting of each phosphorus observation was done by sampling date and then compared with the provided 0.05 mg/L upper guideline. Pros/Cons: Here, the scatter plot clearly depicts each individual measurement allowing us to easily visually compare the differences between the sites, identify the extremely high phosphorus values and the timeline during which the sampling occurred. The con here is that unequal sampling frequency between the sites indicate that the number of points here cannot be understood as a direct measure of how often one site had higher phosphorus than the other. Interpretation: Phosphorus levels were quite varied between the monitoring sites as the largest concentration was identified occurring at the South Saskatchewan River -Hwy 41 site. The highest observed values included 0.505mg/L in 2013, 0.467 mg/L in 2014 and 0.307 mg/L in 2015 where all of them exceed the 0.05 mg/L upper guideline. However, the Bow River sites have much lower levels. Source of Data - Government of Canada

## Visualization 3: Samples outside supplied guidelines

![Samples outside supplied guidelines](Visualizations/Viz3.png)

Rationale: Here, the heat map allows us to compare the frequency of water-quality measurements falling outside the supplied guideline across the 4 sites. Converting each variable to the percentage of samples outside its guidelines provides a common baseline for comparison and the color intensity indicates the higher rates are easy to visually identify. Method: I utilized the measurements from 2013-2016 and excluded the duplicate observations before calculating the proportions. For the variables that have an upper limit, the measurements above that limit were counted and for variables with a lower limit, the measurements below that limit were counted. Then for each variable and the monitoring site, the number of measurements outside the supplied guideline were divided by the total unique measurements and then turned into percentages by multiplying with 100. Pros/Cons: Due to the heat map, we can compare many variables and sites in a single observation. The darker shading allows us to easily identify the fluctuation from the higher concentration guidelines. Utilizing percentage here also provides a common baseline where variables with different measurement units can be compared. However the con here is that the heat map only shows the frequency of these fluctuations from the recommended guidelines, not how much or how far these individual measurements were outside the recommended guidelines. The differences in the sample size among various sites is another factor that limits our understanding of the stability of these percentages. Interpretation: The deviations in guidelines were mainly most re-occurring in the Saskatchewan sites. Saskatchewan - Carrot River possessed the highest percentage of 72.73 % ammonia measurements outside the guideline. Ammonia and Total nitrogen had deviated from the upper guidelines in about 43.9 % of the measurements at South Saskatchewan - Hwy 41. However, the phosphorus deviation at 27% was common in both the Saskatchewan Rivers - Carrot and Hwy 41. However, most variables at the Bow River has no guideline deviation with the exception of ammonia fluctuating from the guideline at Canmore at 7.14 %. Source of Data - Government of Canada

## Visualization 5: Total nitrogen distribution at Highway 41

![Total nitrogen distribution at Highway 41](Visualizations/Viz5.png)

Rationale: The histogram allows us to view the distribution of total nitrogen measurements rather than an average. The measurements are grouped into equal 0.1 mg/L intervals allowing to view the concentration of values, gaps and also the frequency of the concentrations. The 1.0 mg/L guideline allows us to view the distribution in relation to the upper guideline. Method: From the South Saskatchewan River - Hwy 41, total nitrogen measurements from 2013 - 2016 were identified. After the duplicated records were excluded, the 41 measurements were grouped into equal 0.1 mg/L bins and the number of observations in each interval were numbered. Guideline deviations were calculated from the individual measurements with values above 1.0 mg/L identified as exceeding the upper limit. Pros/Cons: The overall shape of the nitrogen distribution is clearly visible with common concentration ranges, less frequent ranges and the void intervals. It is also easy to identify the concentration clusters near the guideline and easier to notice. The con here is that the appearance of the distribution depends on the chosen width of the bin and combining measurements from a lot of years excludes information about the timeline when the individual values occurred. The histogram also depicts the frequency of measurements. Interpretation: Largest concentration of observations can be observed between 1.0 and less than 1.1 mg/L with 8 recorded observations. Overall 18/41 measurements which is about 43.9 % exceeded the upper 1.0 mg/L guideline indicating an increased frequency of elevated nitrogen content at this site. We can also notice the higher values of above 1.4 mg/L however their frequency is lesser. The histogram displays how the measurements are distributed relative to the provided upper guideline.
